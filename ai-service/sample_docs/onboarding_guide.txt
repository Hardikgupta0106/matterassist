Acme Onboarding Guide. Day 1: collect laptop, set up email and Slack, meet your buddy. Week 1: complete security training and read the engineering handbook. Code reviews need one approval, and the main branch is protected. Deployments happen Tuesday and Thursday via the CI pipeline. Every new joiner gives a 15 minute demo of a small starter task at the end of week 2. Probation review happens at 90 days.
