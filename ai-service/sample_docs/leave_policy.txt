Acme Leave Policy. Full-time employees get 24 paid leaves per year, accrued 2 per month. Up to 10 unused leaves carry over to next year. Sick leave is separate: 12 days per year, a medical certificate is needed after 3 consecutive days. Maternity leave is 26 weeks and paternity leave is 2 weeks. Leave requests go through the HR portal and need manager approval at least 3 days in advance.
